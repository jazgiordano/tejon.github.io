French Tile

A European-inspired format that stands out for its distinctive design and its ability to give a roof a unique character.

Model Features

The French model is designed for those seeking a roof with a stronger visual identity. Its format is especially well suited to European, Alpine, or country-style homes.

ProfileFlat with relief
MaterialFired ceramic
FinishNatural terracotta
UseHomes and tourism projects
Guidance Ceramic tiles are sold in pallets of 750 units. The average price is USD 1,550 for French tiles. Quantities, roof pitch, and installation system must be defined according to the project and the existing structure.
At Tejones, we offer a wide variety of ceramic tile designs to suit different architectural styles. From classic models to modern options, each design offers the same quality, strength, and durability, allowing our customers to find the ideal option for every project.
