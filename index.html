Who We Are

Ceramic tradition, local perspective.

Tejones is a company dedicated to manufacturing and marketing high-quality ceramic tiles for homes, buildings, and all types of construction. Its goal is to offer strong, durable products with an attractive design that provide protection and an excellent finish for every project.

Tejones’ plant produces between 4,000 and 6,000 ceramic tiles per month, distributed among the Italian, Roman, and French models. Thanks to its semi-automatic machinery and strict quality control, the company offers strong and durable products. From its factory in Villa General Belgrano, it supplies construction projects and distributors throughout the country.

We aim to offer a simple experience: guidance, a variety of designs, and solutions designed for homes and small construction projects.
